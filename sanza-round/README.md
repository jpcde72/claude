# Sanza Round

The fourth piece. Dawn Set guessed at your music; this one is built from your Spotify listening.
Open `sanza-round.html` (click to play with sound), or watch `sanza-round.mp4`.

**What the listening data showed:** top tracks from Francis Bebey, Circles Around The Sun, Odetta and
the US Navy Band Port Authority Soul Band; top artists including Nightmares On Wax, Herbie Mann and
Medeski, Martin & Wood; recent plays of Ike Turner, Galt MacDermot, Stu Phillips and Moondog; and your

groove-led, dusty, and full of records that producers sample.

**How the piece uses it:**
- One melody in D minor pentatonic, played as a round (after Moondog). Six voices each enter a bar
  after the last: thumb piano (after Bebey's "Sanza Tristesse"), dub bass, Rhodes, organ, flute (after
  Herbie Mann) and bells.
- Each voice draws the same melody as a line in its latitude colour. Sunrise works the same way,
  reaching each latitude at a different time, so the six offset lines stack into the sunrise-delta mark.
- A swung 96 BPM break runs through a dusty sampler (12-bit, half sample rate), shown at the bottom
  and chopped at every bar.
- Bar 9 is a dub drop: everything cuts except bass and drums, the thumb piano's last note echoes into
  the gap, and the snare gets an echo throw. The gaps in the lines are the trims.
- The ending stays modal and doesn't resolve. That's a groove, not a climax.
