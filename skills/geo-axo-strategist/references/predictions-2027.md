# From Being Found to Being Delegated To
## GEO and AXO outlook to end-2027: new thinking, predictions and what brands should do now

*As of 26 September 2026 · v2.0 · Companion to `assets/knowledge.json`*

---

## Executive summary

- **The buyer is becoming software.** In 2026, personal agents moved from demos to consumer scale: Meta Muse (8 Sep), Siri AI on iPhone 18 (Sep), Alexa for Shopping (May), plus checkout in Gemini and AI Mode. The commerce, payment and identity rails needed to let them buy (UCP, ACP, AP2, Visa TAP, Mastercard Agent Pay) are live.
- **GEO is necessary but no longer sufficient.** Being cited (D0) and shortlisted (D1) still matters. But the value is moving to the rungs where the agent **arranges**, **acts** and eventually runs on **autopilot** (D2–D4). At those rungs reliability, protocol support and mandates beat persuasion.
- **2027 winners are being decided by work done in Q4 2026 – H1 2027.** The four moves that pay most: fix agent access and identity, fix the canonical feed, start a per-surface probe baseline, and switch on agent checkout through your PSP. All four are cheap and use existing platforms. Competitors cannot quickly copy the evidence or the time series they create.

## 2. New thinking: ten ideas that update the GEO/AXO model

1. **From ranking to recommendation-set inclusion.** The competitive unit is membership of the 1–3 options an agent considers. Parity makes you eligible; only asymmetric evidence gets you chosen.
2. **Machine availability.** This is the agentic twin of mental availability. Agents choose brands that are easy to resolve, verify and transact. Distinctive assets need machine-legible twins: consistent names, GTINs, claims with evidence and packs a camera can recognise. Ehrenberg-Bass logic still holds for the human who sets the mandate; machine availability covers the agent that executes it.
3. **The Delegation Ladder (D0 Ask → D1 Advise → D2 Arrange → D3 Act → D4 Autopilot).** Each rung changes what wins. Most "GEO" programs stop at D1.
4. **A fourth layer: Mandate.** LDOS optimises Entity, Surface and Protocol. Once a person says "keep me stocked", the default lives in agent memory and is re-audited on service data. **The first delegation is the new first purchase.**
5. **Two-stack reality.** Inside walls (Amazon, and increasingly other large retailers), it is listing plus retail-media optimisation. Outside walls, it is protocol, feed and evidence work. Budget for both.
6. **Paid AXO.** Ads now sit inside agents. Paid units buy *consideration*; evidence still decides the *choice*. Keep paid and earned measured separately.
7. **The evidence economy.** Third-party grounding share is the durable GEO metric. Expect engines to start discounting pay-to-play listicles (low-confidence prediction P12).
8. **Agent identity is the new robots.txt.** The question shifts from "which crawlers may read" to "which verified agents, acting for which people, may transact".
9. **The inbox as evidence.** Muse reads Gmail and Siri reads Mail. Order confirmations, delivery updates and service emails become machine-read proof of reliability.
10. **Brand agents meet buyer agents.** The website becomes one interface of several. A grounded brand agent or MCP endpoint answers buying agents with authority. Negotiation between agents starts in travel, insurance and B2B.

## 3. Predictions to end-2027

Confidence levels: **high** means we would bet on it; **medium** means more likely than not; **low** means plausible and worth watching. Each prediction names the signals that would confirm or falsify it.

| # | Prediction | When | Conf. | Watch for | So what |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Meta puts paid units in Muse (sponsored recommendations or connectors), sold via Advantage+ | H1 2027 | High | Ad tests, earnings commentary | Meta catalogue quality becomes Muse eligibility |
| P2 | All top-4 consumer assistants support agent checkout or hand-off; PSPs ship UCP↔ACP bridges | 2027 | High | Stripe, Adyen, PayPal, Shopify notes | Two rails via one PSP is near-free distribution |
| P3 | Walled gardens harden; retailers charge "agent tolls" and sell agent-era retail media | 2027 | High | ToS changes, retailer agent APIs | Plan two stacks and an in-wall budget |
| P4 | Verified agent identity becomes a CDN and PSP default; unverified bot checkout is blocked | H2 2027 | Medium | Cloudflare and Akamai launches, network mandates | Bot misconfiguration becomes the top invisibility cause |
| P5 | Standing mandates reach mainstream use in grocery, household, pet and consumables | 2027 | Medium | Alexa Scheduled Actions, Muse and Instacart | FMCG share moves to mandate moments |
| P6 | Agents do first-round B2B shortlisting for most software and services purchases | 2027 | High | IDC and Gartner studies, procurement launches | Trust centre and pricing logic become growth assets |
| P7 | Share of answer and share of shortlist enter trackers and MMM | 2027 | Medium | Tracker vendors' AI modules | A 2026 baseline is a moat |
| P8 | Brand agents become a standard ad destination (Sponsored Agents, click-to-WhatsApp AI) | 2027 | Medium | Adoption data | Govern the brand agent like the website |
| P9 | Rules on disclosure of sponsored agent recommendations and liability for agent errors | 2027 | Medium | EU Digital Fairness Act, FTC | Accurate structured claims become compliance |
| P10 | Camera-first agents make packs, shelves and OOH machine-read discovery paths | 2027 | Medium | Glasses sales, visual search share | Distinctive assets must be model-recognisable |
| P11 | 40%+ of brand-side agent projects cancelled; data foundations win | 2027 | High | Write-downs, vendor consolidation | Entity and feeds before bespoke agents |
| P12 | Engines discount pay-to-play listicles; verified reviews gain weight | 2027 | Low | Citation-mix shift in probe data | Build verified evidence, not placement volume |

**What would make us wrong.** A major agent trust failure (fraud or a privacy scandal at scale) could slow D3–D4 adoption by 12–18 months. Even then, nothing in the action list is wasted. Entity, feed, evidence and access work all pay off in classical search and retail media too.

### A 90-day starter plan
- **Weeks 1–2:** A1 access audit · A11 agent segment · pick the 6 surfaces and 40 probes.
- **Weeks 3–6:** A3 first probe runs (3 reps each) · A2 feed gap analysis · ARI v2 baseline.
- **Weeks 7–10:** A2 fixes shipped · A4 enable one rail through the PSP · A10 App Intents backlog.
- **Weeks 11–13:** Re-probe · journey tests per surface · board readout: ARI v2 components, share of answer by surface, 2027 plan (A5–A12).

### Organisation
Name one **AXO owner** with a cross-functional squad (ecommerce, search, brand, CX, payments, legal, analytics). AXO fails when it is treated as an SEO sub-task, because most of the levers sit outside search teams.
