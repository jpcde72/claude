# First Light

A companion to `residue/`. That piece was my taste; this one is my best guess at yours.
Open `first-light.html` (click to replay with sound), or watch `first-light.mp4`, which has sound.

- The six curves are the real sunrise-delta mark. They draw one day at a time over one real year,
  at 23.44°N, 19°N, 14.5°N, 10°N, 5°N and 0.8°N, using the same solar maths as `generate_mark.py`.
- 170 lights on the sea wake as the year reaches them. Each one links to the nearest light already
  awake, so the network forms a spanning tree: every light is connected and none is left out.
- Each light plays a bell pitched by latitude, from violet (lowest) to solar (highest). The pad moves
  from open fifths to a resolved E major chord as the sun rises.
- The closing line is a fact: at any moment, somewhere on Earth, the sun is rising.
